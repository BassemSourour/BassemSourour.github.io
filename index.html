About

A bit more

I like building things that solve real problems. The part I enjoy most is the stretch before any code gets written — working out what's actually wrong, who it's a problem for, and what a fix would need to look like to be worth using. Everything I've built that I'm happy with started there.

I've just finished second year at Guelph. Most of what I've built outside of coursework has been small tools and creative coding projects, and this past summer was the first time I wrote software that other people depended on to do their jobs — which changed how I think about most of it.
