About

A bit more

A lot of that summer was spent away from a keyboard — walking the plant, asking operators to show me what they actually do, and drawing how information moved between them. The building came afterwards, and it was better for it.

Before this summer everything I'd written was coursework or small projects for myself. It was the first time people depended on something I built to get through a shift, and it changed what I think "finished" means.
